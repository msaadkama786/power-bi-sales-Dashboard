## project Insight
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Discount across different business dimensions.
Achieved Total Sales of 2.30M, Total Profit of 286.40K, 38K Quantity Sold, and 1.56K Total Discount.
Consumer segment contributed the highest share of overall sales, followed by Corporate and Home Office segments.
The West region generated the highest revenue (approximately 31.6% of total sales), indicating strong regional performance.
Phones, Chairs, and Storage emerged as the top-selling sub-categories, while Labels and Fasteners contributed the least.
California recorded the highest number of sales transactions, followed by New York and Texas.
Time-series analysis identified fluctuations in sales over the order period, helping detect seasonal demand patterns.
Interactive visuals, slicers, KPI cards, and drill-down capabilities enabled faster business decision-making.
